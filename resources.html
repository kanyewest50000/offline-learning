Glossary

The handful of terms that come up in every deployment conversation.

Content pack
A self-contained subject bundle — lessons, media, assessments, teacher companion — that runs fully offline.
Sync hub
A device or small server that aggregates progress and distributes pack updates during a network window.
Opportunistic sync
Exchanging data whenever a network happens to be reachable, rather than on a fixed schedule.
Mesh seeding
One synced device sharing packs peer-to-peer with others in the room, no internet involved.
Mastery gate
An offline-scored checkpoint a learner must clear before the next strand unlocks.
Eventual sync
The guarantee that an offline record reaches a hub across some future network contact, not immediately.
Untether

Education infrastructure for the 2.6 billion people beyond reliable connectivity. Content packs, mesh sync, and rugged classroom hardware that deliver a full curriculum with zero bars of signal — and catch up the moment any device touches the network.
